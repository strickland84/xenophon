# Security

The game runs entirely in the browser and talks to other players over public Nostr relays. A pilot's identity is a
key pair kept in the browser.

If you find a way to take over another player's pilot, read or forge their save, crash other players' games over the
network, or anything else that could hurt players, please **do not open a public issue**. Report it privately through
[a security advisory](https://github.com/strickland84/xenophon/security/advisories/new). You will get an answer
within a week.

Cheating at the game itself (a player lying about their own ship) is a known limit of a game with no server, and is
fine to discuss in an ordinary issue.
