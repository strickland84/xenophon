The **assets** are not. Copyright © 2026 James Strickland. All rights reserved. They are:

- every picture, sprite, model and font: everything under `art/` and `static/` that is not code, and `docs/art/`;
- the generated art written into the code by the tools: the ship models (`src/lib/engine/jet/models/`), and the
  `art.ts`, `prefabs.ts` and `placeart.ts` files under `src/lib/engine/`;
- the sound effects and the narrator's voice (`static/audio/sfx/`, `static/audio/voice/`, `art/audio/`, but for the

Contributions to the assets are welcome, and are accepted on these same terms.

Some assets were made with third-party models and services, each under its own licence (the implementation plan's
section 7 lists them): images and sound effects by local open models through ComfyUI; the narrator's voice by ElevenLabs on the
maintainer's paid plan.

Not covered: **the music** (`art/audio/music/`, rendered into `static/audio/music/`, and `static/licences/`) is other
people's work, each piece under its own licence (Freedoom's BSD licence, CC-BY-SA 3.0, OGA-BY 3.0, CC-BY, public
domain): `art/audio/music/README.md` lists them, and the game names them in Settings, Sound.
