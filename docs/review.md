# A fresh review (18 November 2026)

One reviewer who built none of it, playing the game as James would, in a real browser at 1366 × 768 from a fresh save,
then at 844 × 390 (a phone held sideways) for the main paths. Pictures are in [art/gen/review/](art/gen/review/); a
name with `after` is the same place once fixed.

## How it was played

About an hour of game time on a laptop-sized page, offline (`?net=off`): some 25 minutes by hand through the page's keys
and mouse, about 35 more flown by a script steering the mouse on five frontier worlds (fighting whatever came, mining
between) while the review was written, seven of those sat under the keep-safe reminder on a volcanic world (fault 46).
By hand: the title, callsign, side and the pilot
creator (every race); the whole first sortie by its prompts with the keys and the mouse (crashed twice on the way, as a
new player would); the base's screen and every tab; the hangar, the bench (both tabs), the booth (every tab and every
pattern), the vault; the deck, the shop (every tab), buying a Galleon; aboard the Galleon, buying and placing furniture;
the menu and every panel and tab (faction board, pilots, sheet, squad, your pilot, settings and its five sections), chat,
both maps and a card; flight over eleven kinds of world and weather in all three views (archipelago, continental, mesa,
ice, volcanic, ocean, jungle, a moon, night, sunset, a storm); space by a world, a moon, the sun, a gas giant, a black
hole, a belt, a station and a gate in all three views; up out of a world from behind, down into one overhead, docking,
the cruise drive; frontier fights; the `/sounds` page (nobody here can hear it). Phone:
`tools/phone-check.mjs` walked 43 screens and its pictures were looked at; the side, the first prompt, a base, flying,
from behind and on foot are in the list below. Every other hull's rooms were judged from `art/gen/tiles.png` and
`fit-outs.png` with the Galleon's in the game.

## What it is like

The worlds are the best thing in it: from overhead and from behind, an archipelago at noon, a mesa, an ice sheet or a
lava field is lovely, and the way down from space is a real moment. The interface's look holds together. But the first
five minutes are where a new player decides, and they are rough: the first sortie's own instructions run him into the
ground, its last step can stall with no word why, its box covers the very names it is announcing, and a crash is called
being shot down. Inside, the hangar and the rooms are honest work spoiled by things that make no sense: the Galleon pokes
through the hangar's back wall, the wall-side furniture is static, a berth is a red box, and the bench hid the ship it was
fitting. In flight the player's own ship, from behind, reads as a dark lump with icicles under it.

## Left, and what each needs

- **Wall-side furniture (10). Done:** every built-in piece's side and back facings drawn again by the model over a guide
  made from its own front facing (its top as seen from above, turned to the new facing, its end or back a panel below:
  `tools/gen/interiors.py --from-front`), each try judged as the game has it (in its front's colours, its specks taken
  out: `tiling.py clean`) for noise and likeness to its front (`noise`, `unlike`), and the cleanest passing try of every
  facing chosen; `tools/voxel/interiors.py` refuses one that is still noise. The tall one-tile pieces (lockers, shelves,
  trophy cases) would not draw that narrow: theirs are their old tries, cleaned.
- **The ship from behind (4). Done** (the rebuild after this review): each hull's plan is a solid, drawn by the model
  from the side and from behind as well as from above, carved and lit by face; see the plan's section 7.
- **The hangar by hull (9, 12). Done:** the hangar is laid out round the ship it holds (its bay grown by repeating plain
  bands of its plates), and every place inside is drawn at one art pixel, followed like a ship's rooms. Left: the hangar
  doors are one picture, narrower than a Galleon.
- **The heat's edge.** Close to a sun the frame's edges are ringed in a dotted orange band, the same picture frame the
  way down had (3): it wants the oval the way down now has.
- **The side cards (16). Done:** drawn by the game (`tools/side-cards.ts`), the side's own station and ships.
- The rest of the minor list: each an hour or less.

## Fixed, and how it was checked

Commits `4bede1e` and `ed9ccf7`. Each fix was looked at again in the game (the pictures marked `after`), the frames
it changes re-recorded on purpose (49 flights, then 20 that land by a base), lint, the type check and the suite passing.
`CRASH_COMMIT` (data/hulls.ts) and `LAND.padPull` (data/loop.ts) are the new numbers.
